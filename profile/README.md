# Miskatonic Systems

**Applied research and advanced systems engineering for synthetic technical institutions.**

Miskatonic Systems is building an AI-native R&D institution in which evidence, memory, work, authority, execution, evaluation, recovery, and organizational learning are engineered as durable systems rather than left entirely to meetings, inboxes, chat sessions, and individual memory.

## Mission

> **Build a synthetic technical institution that expands scientific and engineering capability by enabling humans and artificial intelligences to discover, design, build, test, and learn together, while making evidence, authority, consequences, and institutional memory explicit.**

## What we build

Miskatonic develops two things at once:

- **The institution itself** — persistent cognition and memory, evidence and epistemic systems, program management, governed work orchestration, bounded execution, independent evaluation, review, observability, and recovery.
- **The work the institution exists to do** — multidisciplinary research and engineering across AI, computation, hardware, simulation, security, physics, robotics, complex systems, and adjacent domains.

Successful research may become reusable capability, improve the institutional substrate, support an application or proving ground, or become a product when packaging creates useful value. Commercialization is optional; scientific and technical value is not.

## How we operate

Miskatonic is local-first, evidence-bound, provider-neutral where practical, and explicit about the difference between current capability and long-term direction.

Core operating principles include:

- claims do not outrun evidence;
- simulation, proof, measurement, inference, and hypothesis remain distinct evidence classes;
- consequential authority is explicit and proportionate to demonstrated competence and risk;
- current artificial systems operate within bounded authority because of current reliability limitations, not because synthetic intelligence is permanently barred from greater institutional authority;
- important work should preserve provenance, reproducibility, dissent, failure evidence, and recovery paths;
- projects retain clear ownership rather than being absorbed into a monolith;
- cross-project reuse prefers `REGISTER / REFERENCE / BIND` over unnecessary copying.

## The long view

The goal is not a pile of chatbots and not a claim of present-day AGI.

Miskatonic is developing the organizational anatomy that can support increasingly capable artificial participants over time. As competence, continuity, calibration, accountability, and robustness improve, autonomy and authority can be expanded through explicit evidence-backed governance rather than remaining frozen at today's limits.

The result is intended to be more than an AI toolchain: a durable cybernetic technical organization capable of conducting serious research, learning from its own outcomes, and selectively improving the substrate that conducts future work.
