# Security Policy

Miskatonic Systems welcomes good-faith vulnerability and safety reports.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Report privately through **GitHub Private Vulnerability Reporting** on either of our
public repositories:

- [`Miskatonic-System/.github`](https://github.com/Miskatonic-System/.github/security/advisories/new)
- [`Miskatonic-System/miskatonic-review-authority`](https://github.com/Miskatonic-System/miskatonic-review-authority/security/advisories/new)

Open the repository's **Security** tab and choose *Report a vulnerability*. A report about
any Miskatonic component may be filed through either repository; routing to the component
owner happens internally.

> **Do not report vulnerabilities in public issues, discussions, or pull requests.**
> A public issue is not a private report. If the details are already public, say so in
> your private report so timing can be handled realistically.

Most Miskatonic repositories are private and cannot receive reports directly. That is a
property of their visibility, not an invitation to publish elsewhere.

## What is in scope

Reports are welcome about:

- vulnerabilities in published Miskatonic code, artifacts, or configuration;
- exposure of credentials, secrets, or private data in anything we publish;
- safety or misuse concerns about published capability;
- integrity problems affecting published evidence or provenance.

## What helps

- enough detail for safe reproduction;
- the affected component, version, commit, or configuration where known;
- observed versus expected behaviour;
- your assessment of impact, even a rough one.

## What we ask

- avoid unnecessary destructive testing;
- avoid accessing, modifying, or exfiltrating data that is not yours;
- avoid degrading service for others;
- allow reasonable mitigation time before publication where coordinated disclosure
  applies.

## What you can expect

Miskatonic does not currently staff a 24/7 security desk, and we would rather tell you
that than publish a response time we cannot meet.

Instead of a fixed service level, we commit to **state transitions**. A report moves
through intake, acknowledgment, assessment, and disposition, and we will tell you which
state your report is in and what it is waiting on.

We do not request indefinite secrecy for convenience.

Nothing in this policy is a legal commitment, and we do not make safe-harbor promises
that have not been separately authorized.

## Credit

We are glad to credit reporters where appropriate and desired. You may also ask to remain
anonymous.

## Coordinated disclosure

Where a fix, a third party, or an upstream project is involved, we coordinate release
timing with the affected owners rather than disclosing around them. A fix being available
is not by itself authorization to release details.
